# 📊 Volume & Relative Volume (RVOL) Confirmation Protocol

**Version:** 11.0 (2026-08-26) — see docs/CHANGELOG.md for the canonical version history.


## 1. Introduction
Volume represents the total quantity of an asset traded during a specified timeframe. In institutional trading frameworks, price action reveals *where* participants are moving, while volume reveals the *intensity* and *credibility* of that movement. 

This strategy uses Volume and Relative Volume (RVOL) as a foundational confirmation gate. It assumes that price movements without institutional volume support are highly susceptible to market manipulation or immediate exhaustion. Consequently, no breakout or trend-following entry is considered valid under this framework without confirmed volume expansion.

---

## 2. Technical Structure and Calculations
To evaluate the statistical validity of trading volume, the raw volume must be compared against a rolling historical baseline.

### 2.1 Core Components
*   **Raw Volume ($V_t$):** The total volume of transactions executed within the current candle.
*   **Average Volume ($AV_t$):** A 20-period Simple Moving Average (SMA) of raw volume, representing the historical baseline:
    $$AV_t = \frac{1}{20} \sum_{i=0}^{19} V_{t-i}$$
*   **Relative Volume ($RVOL_t$):** The ratio of the current volume to the average volume, quantifying institutional participation:
    $$RVOL_t = \frac{V_t}{AV_t}$$

### 2.2 Volume Regime Classifications
The operational state of volume is divided into four distinct trading regimes:

| Regime | Mathematical Condition | Market Context | Strategic Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consolidation** | $RVOL_t < 1.0$ | Low retail and institutional interest. | Breakthrough or breakout signals are rejected. Range-bound mean-reversion is expected. |
| **Normal** | $1.0 \le RVOL_t < 1.5$ | Average liquidity; typical day-to-day market conditions. | Standard setups are executed with basic risk parameters. |
| **Institutional** | $RVOL_t \ge 1.5$ | Heavy institutional commitment. Big blocks are being filled. | Required validation for breakouts, Squeeze Momentum releases, S/R breaks, and MACD crossovers (all require $RVOL \ge 1.5$). Sizing can be scaled up. |
| **Exhaustion Climax** | $RVOL_t \ge 3.0$ | Climax volume. Extreme participation — mean-reversion expectation. | High probability of trend exhaustion. No new entries; used to tighten stop-losses or execute immediate exits. |

## 3. Operational Inception & Breakout Validation Gates

The volume confirmation protocol acts as a gatekeeper for all entry signals generated by momentum indicators (MACD, Squeeze Momentum) and market structure levels.

### 3.1 Support/Resistance Breakout Gate
When the price closes decisively beyond an established horizontal support ($S_1, S_2$) or resistance ($R_1, R_2$) line:
*   **Confirmed Breakout:** The breakout candle must close with $RVOL_t \ge 1.5$. This confirms that institutions are committing capital to drive price into the new value area.
*   **Fakeout (Rejected):** If the breakout candle close occurs with $RVOL_t < 1.5$, the move is classified as a low-volume "head fake." The breakout is invalidated, and positions are not opened.

### 3.2 Momentum Crossover Confirmation
*   **MACD Crossover Validation:** A MACD bullish crossover occurring below the zero line is only accepted if the crossover candle (or the immediate subsequent candle) prints $RVOL_t \ge 1.5$ (the canonical institutional threshold), confirming capital inflow. A previous version of this section specified $\ge 1.2$ which was inconsistent with the §2 institutional band; the canonical value is $1.5$.
*   **Squeeze Release Validation:** When the Squeeze Momentum indicator transitions to **Squeeze OFF** (triggering a release), the trigger candle must print $RVOL_t \ge 1.5$ for the breakout to be considered valid.
*   **Momentum Oscillator Divergence Confirmation (RSI, OBV, CMF, MFI, Stochastic, MACD, ChandeMO, Squeeze):** the divergence-trigger candle must break the nearest S/R level by > 0.2% — no separate RVOL check is required. The geometric divergence (peak/trough) + the structural S/R break together form the confirmation. The canonical divergence-bearing set is `rsi`, `stochastic`, `chandemo`, `macd`, `obv`, `cmf`, `mfi`, `squeeze` — eight parent indicators with `supports_divergence: true` per the registry manifest ([04-02-00-indicator-index.md](../indicators/04-02-00-indicator-index.md)).

## 4. Volume Divergences

Analyzing the divergence between price direction and volume trend helps identify emerging structural weaknesses before they appear on price charts.

### 4.1 Bearish Volume Divergence
*   **Structure:** Price continues to make higher highs, but corresponding volume peaks make lower lows (under declining $RVOL$).
*   **Interpretation:** The upward move is exhausting, driven by thin liquidity rather than active institutional buying. It suggests a high-probability reversal is approaching.

### 4.2 Bullish Volume Divergence
*   **Structure:** Price continues to make lower lows, but volume peaks decline significantly, showing decreasing selling pressure.
*   **Interpretation:** The markdown phase is losing institutional backing, indicating potential seller exhaustion and accumulation near the lows.

## Normalization

**Volume**: Always 0.0 normalized (raw-only chart series that never influences the directional confluence engine).

**RVOL**: Discrete 4-band mapping (per the v2.1 contract in [04-02-19-rvol.md §3](../indicators/04-02-19-rvol.md)):

| RVOL range | `values.rvol_band` | Label | Participation regime |
|------------|-------------------|-------|----------------------|
| < 1.0 | −0.5 | `LOW_PARTICIPATION_VOLUME` | Low participation — breakouts rejected |
| 1.0 – 1.5 | 0.2 | `NORMAL_PARTICIPATION_VOLUME` | Average liquidity |
| 1.5 – 3.0 | 0.8 | `INSTITUTIONAL_BREAKOUT_VOLUME` | Institutional commitment — breakouts validated |
| ≥ 3.0 | −1.0 | `EXHAUSTION_CLIMAX_VOLUME` | Mean-reversion climax — no new entries |

The signed 4-band value lives in **`IndicatorEvaluation.values.rvol_band`**, **not** in `IndicatorEvaluation.normalized`. Per the v2.1 non-directional gate contract (mirrored in the BBWP convention), the canonical `IndicatorEvaluation.normalized` field is **always `0.0`** for RVOL. A previous version of this section assigned the signed band value directly to `normalized`, contradicting the canonical gate contract in [04-02-19-rvol.md](../indicators/04-02-19-rvol.md).

Both Volume and RVOL are non-directional gates. Confidence is computed separately from `normalized` and reads from the per-indicator base+signal-boost formulas in [04-02-18-volume.md §Confidence](#confidence) and [04-02-19-rvol.md](../indicators/04-02-19-rvol.md).

**Gate exclusion rule (v2.1 — clarified).** The `IndicatorEvaluation.normalized` field of a non-directional gate is **never added to the flat directional confluence sum** (the sum of trend/momentum/volume/volatility votes). For RVOL the signed band value in `values.rvol_band` (`−0.5 … 0.8`, `−1.0`) is consumed only as a **scalar gate coefficient** — a confidence/participation multiplier applied to *other* indicators' confirmed signals (breakout validation, squeeze release, S/R breaks). The band sign encodes participation regime (institutional vs exhaustion), not price direction, so it must not register as a bullish/bearish voter. Confluence-engine implementations must read the gate via `values.rvol_band`, not the directional `normalized` accumulator.

> **Label consistency.** The lowest-band label `LOW_PARTICIPATION_VOLUME` is the canonical term carried in the runtime `state_label` of `IndicatorEvaluation`, harmonized with the RVOL spec ([04-02-19-rvol.md](../indicators/04-02-19-rvol.md)). All contract consumers (regex/policy string matching, property tests, downstream panels) read this exact string.
